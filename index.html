Pugnare.health

After being diagnosed with T2, I found out how much metabolic disease drugs can cost. The manufacturers create the problem and then the solution, at least for privately (poorly) insured patients: coupons for each individual drug. 

I started a free website, hosted on github pages, open source, no tracking. It just groups and allows some quickly navigable information about some of the most expensive of these drugs including GLP-1s and insulin. My dietician has been sharing it with fellow patients.
